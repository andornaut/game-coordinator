## Scope

Apply the [shared focus appearance rule](../design/game-policies.md#controls-and-input) to game controls and mocks. Ada's purchase Cancel mock is the reference example. Game Kit owns shared control primitives that supply the outline. Existing game owners own caller styles and checks.

## Criteria

- Remove generic browser-style focus rings from the ordinary game presentation. Use a visibly distinct keyboard-focus state in the game's control styling and preserve visible focus in forced-colors modes.
- Preserve native focus, tab order, keyboard activation, focus restoration and the separate appearance of pressed, selected, disabled and ordinary graphic borders.
- Inspect actual pointer and keyboard routes and modal dismissal, including visible restoration. Record the affected shared primitive or caller, the checked appearance, and normal delivery or the exact blocker.

## Outcome

Ada shipped its focus treatment in `f0446ad0` (ada-0340), Archie in `492b90ff`
(adw-0184), and Chibi in `3edf5ed0` (cwh-0273). Owner checks covered keyboard
and pointer routes, visible forced-colors focus, and modal restoration. Ada
verified purchase Cancel returned focus to the original offer. Archie checked
its cyan focus and system-color treatments. Chibi checked Menu, skill and
recovery controls, including keyboard Retry. The checked scope includes
browser and emulated layouts. Ada's physical display cutouts remain unverified.
