## Scope

All game content outside the top modal, including top controls, HUD and lower cards, stays painted and laid out, dimmed and inactive.

## Criteria

- A background click invokes the existing Cancel or Back once when the modal is closeable; mandatory choices stay nondismissible.

## Decisions

None recorded.

## Outcome

Policy live in [Modal background isolation](../game-policies.md#modal-background-isolation) as `c136eda1`. Consumer implementation is tracked separately.
