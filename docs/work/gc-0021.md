## Scope

Apply the [Updates policy](../game-policies.md#updates) in Ada the Navigator, Archie and Chibi.
Game Tech owners handle their lifecycle and modal integration. Game Kit owns the shared updater.

## Criteria

- Verify a check on launch, start of play, return to Start/Home and foreground return from another app.
  Coalesce overlapping lifecycle signals and verify that the download and staging are silent.
- Verify the existing update modal on Start/Home, during active play and over a paused or modal view.
  Cancel restores the exact view, focus and pause state. Update now saves as applicable and reloads
  into the verified staged build. An already staged ordinary update still requires confirmation
  on cold launch.
- Preserve offline operation, cache and build integrity, and explicitly approved newer-save recovery.
  Report exact qualified source or existing compliance, real browser evidence and normal delivery.
