## Scope

Roll out the [shared button rule](../game-policies.md#controls-and-input) to every game. Game Kit owns the rendering primitives and control behavior. Existing Tech owners own their game migrations and coordinate appearance and behavior verification with Art and Gameplay.

## Criteria

- Inventory standard text and icon buttons across menus, modals, HUDs and standalone controls, including loading-safe Retry. Use shared Game Kit construction and guard against new bypasses.
- Preserve game skins, labels, placement, callbacks and navigation. Keep continuous steering, dragging, sliders and editing on their existing paths. Reuse existing focus, hold and modal work.
- Record the exact public Game Kit pin, source revision and inventory disposition. Verify representative keyboard, pointer, touch, hold and forced-colors behavior and modal focus restoration. Collect normal gate, CI, deployment and applicable live acceptance evidence.
