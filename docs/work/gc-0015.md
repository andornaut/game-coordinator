## Scope

Keep a bounded window of closed messages per recipient, ordered by close sequence. Coordination Kit owns compaction. The coordinator reviews the first live dry run and enables pruning at an existing review boundary after qualification.

## Criteria

- Size the window from the live distribution of message counts and bytes per recipient. A busy recipient cannot consume another recipient's window. Eligibility does not depend on calendar age.
- Consider only handled or superseded originals outside the window. Keep open, retained, disputed and unresolved messages and their dependencies. Treat missing ownership or evidence as a reason to keep the original.
- Preserve designs, decisions, approvals and material evidence in their owning records. If a message supplies the sole copy or a live reference still requires its original body, protect it until the owner records a verified durable replacement.
- Keep a minimal ID and disposition record for deduplication, supersession and reference resolution. Expiration must remove the payload from replayable active history, including duplicate projections. Keep independent backups under their recovery policy.
- Provide a read-only dry run by recipient with eligible and protected counts, bytes and reasons. Qualify rebuild, restore, concurrent writes, retry and interrupted compaction before operating on live data. Preserve guards, the message lifecycle and normal delivery. Use the current review cadence.
