## Scope

Apply the [procedural systems](../design/game-policies.md#procedural-systems) and
[controls and input](../design/game-policies.md#controls-and-input) conventions in Ada the Navigator, Archie and
Chibi through their Tech owners. Game Kit owns the shared components, `randomStream` and the spacing
tokens.

## Criteria

- Each game records its remaining gaps as work items linked to gc-0028 and closes them in normal delivery.
- Game Kit confirms the listed components, `randomStream(seed, name)` and the 16/8/24 px spacing tokens are
  published, or ships what is missing.
- Chibi retires its trace and plan hashes as their refactors finish.
