## Scope

Every Pause or Menu outside Start/title shows Back to title immediately above its bottom Resume or Close, including Ada's Earth narrative Menu; ordinary dialogs and choice cards are unchanged.

## Criteria

- Back to title returns to Title while preserving save, progression, settlement and exit-confirmation guards.

None recorded.
