## Scope

Coordination Kit owns the app registry, configurable roles and explicit permissions.
Delivery Kit owns app-scoped delivery and its reporting contract. The coordinator owns
compatibility qualification and migration of the existing games workspace.

## Criteria

- The public kit interfaces use app terminology. Workflows accept role IDs from configuration.
  A single app with a single arbitrary role can coordinate work and run delivery.
- Privileged operations require explicit permissions bound to the active registered caller.
  Arbitrary role names confer no authority, and one role may hold all required permissions.
- Accept existing config, CLI and event formats during the upgrade. Preserve role and app IDs,
  queued work and decisions, store history, state directories, runner locks and event cursors.
- Qualify a fresh solo app, multiple apps with different role sets, and the existing games
  workspace. Exercise authorized and denied actions, mixed-version reporting, and both
  scheduled and ad hoc delivery. Publish Coordination Kit first, then Delivery Kit.
- Adopt the checked pins through existing owner boundaries and verify actual readback before
  changing the live workspace's configuration. Record legacy alias retirement separately
  after compatible readers and writers are in place.
