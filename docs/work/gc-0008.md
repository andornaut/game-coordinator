## Scope

Every discrete click or tap control performs the same action on hold unless a different hold action is bound; a hold fires once and suppresses the release click.

## Criteria

- Overrides, availability, scopes, cancellation, disposal and continuous, drag and editing exceptions are preserved.

## Decisions

None recorded.

## Outcome

Policy live in [Controls and input](../game-policies.md#controls-and-input) as `240d5f5b`. `7bf3ea5` later set the shared hold duration: a hold on a discrete control fires after 500 ms in every game, and Archie keeps its tuned timing only for untargeted drag input. Per-game migration is tracked separately.
