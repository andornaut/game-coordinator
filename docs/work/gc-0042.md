## Scope

Apply the [Back, Close and Resume policy](../design/game-policies.md#back-close-and-resume)
to mobile browser Back and installed PWA system Back in all games.

## Criteria

- During active, pausable play, platform Back may open Pause or start the existing End walk flow with its guards.
- After an explicit return all the way to home or Start, the next platform Back follows the platform and leaves on the first press, without a trap or extra confirmation.
- Preserve one-layer Back from nested menus and pages and the separate paths for Escape, in-game buttons and letterbox dismissal.
- Verify both mobile browser and installed PWA behavior where supported.

## Outcome

Adoption verification is pending.
