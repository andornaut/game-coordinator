## Scope

The recipient prompts the user before acting on an original whose sender identity
the coordination store could not verify. Coordination Kit owns enforcement and
published runtime support. The coordinator owns the shared policy and consumer
qualification.

## Criteria

- Preserve the original and its unverified identity. At the next safe boundary,
  open one structured user decision per exact original, with a recommendation
  first. A repeated read or notification must preserve the existing decision.
- Permit action only after an explicit user decision covering that original.
  Approval is limited to the scope the user chooses, and ordinary authorization
  checks still apply. Record an explicit decline against the original.
- Keep the decision pending if the prompt cannot be shown. Preserve unrelated
  work, safeguards, questions and holds.
- Verify existing open and new originals, repeated reads, restart, and verified
  sender behavior through the checked kit and existing coordination paths.

## Outcome

The shared communication policy applies at recipient review. Kit enforcement and
consumer verification are pending.
