## Scope

Apply the [rectangular button lip rule](../design/game-policies.md#rectangular-button-lips)
across all games in designs, mockups, HTML/CSS, canvas and skins.

## Criteria

- When a dark lip is visible, it forms one solid rectangle flush to both sides.
- Normal, pressed and disabled silhouettes keep straight lower edges and hard square corners, with no gaps or show-through.
- If a press intentionally hides the lip, the remaining face retains a straight lower edge and square lower corners.
- Verify and record actual raster output at normal and enlarged scales.
