## Scope

Coordination Kit owns the native worker collector. The coordinator verifies the published
fix with the installed kit and existing review runner.

## Criteria

- A slow or unreadable worker cannot erase verified evidence for other parents and children.
- Reports distinguish verified observations, partial coverage and unavailable reads. A bounded
  read never implies full historical queue coverage or question clearance.
- Verify the change against the recurring native timeouts with a bounded collector proof and
  a genuine scheduled review. Preserve verified lineage, original messages, guards and review cadence.

## Outcome

Coordination Kit 7.3.1 distinguished partial coverage from unavailable reads. Coordination Kit
7.3.2 rotated the bounded sample across genuine reviews. The first 7.3.2 review matched all 14
parent bindings and verified 83 children, including 76 absent from the previous sample.

Coverage remains partial for all 14 parents. The review recorded 303 child reads outside usable
coverage and routed two verified historical failures to their owners. The coordinator preserved
the runners, delivery controls and store checkpoint.
